Portfolio VaR & Expected Shortfall Engine

A live market-risk engine using Historical, Parametric and Monte Carlo VaR, Expected Shortfall and backtesting.

Open Live Risk Engine →

Objective

Measure portfolio market risk at configurable confidence levels and time horizons using current market data.

Methodology

MethodApproach
Historical VaRUses the empirical distribution of realized portfolio returns.
Parametric VaRUses portfolio mean, volatility and the Normal quantile.
Monte Carlo VaRSimulates portfolio returns using estimated mean and volatility.
Expected ShortfallAverage loss beyond the VaR cutoff.

Validation

The live dashboard performs rolling VaR backtesting and compares realized losses against the model threshold.

Business Meaning

The model answers three risk-management questions: how much could the portfolio lose under normal conditions, how severe are losses beyond VaR, and does the model produce an acceptable number of exceptions?

Model Limitations

Historical relationships can change during stressed markets. Parametric assumptions can understate fat tails, and free quote sources may be delayed. Outputs are analytical demonstrations rather than trading or regulatory risk reports.
