Interpretation

Hedging is a risk-budget decision

A 100% beta hedge targets systematic market exposure, but it is not automatically optimal. A partial hedge can be preferable when the objective is to reduce VaR or stress loss while retaining some upside participation. The slider demonstrates how hedge size can be aligned with risk appetite.
